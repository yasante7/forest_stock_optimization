## AI Detection Evasion Techniques Applied

### 1. **Sentence Dynamics (Burstiness)**
- Mixed very short sentences (5-8 words) with complex-compound sentences (40+ words)
- Used em-dashes, semicolons, and parentheses for non-linear thought structures
- Occasional rhetorical questions where appropriate
- Example: "Can we quantify the trade-offs?" followed by detailed 3-sentence explanation

### 2. **Vocabulary Control (Eliminated AI-isms)**
**Forbidden words REMOVED:**
- ❌ "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", "Notably"
- ❌ "It is important to note that"
- ❌ "Pivotal", "Comprehensive", "Crucial", "Innovative", "Tapestry", "Multifaceted"
- ❌ "Delve into", "Underscore", "Highlight", "Showcase"

**Replaced with:**
- ✅ Direct transitions: "This pattern...", "Yet...", "But..."
- ✅ Discipline-specific verbs: "isolates the variable", "demonstrates", "reveals"
- ✅ Technical precision: "quantify", "estimate", "calibrate"

### 3. **Tone and Voice**
- **Active voice predominates**: "Farmers clear 58,000 hectares" vs "58,000 hectares are cleared"
- **Specific hedging**: "The data suggests a correlation, albeit one limited by spatial resolution constraints" instead of "It could be argued that..."
- **Intentional stylistic idiosyncrasies**: Occasional fragments for emphasis, varied paragraph lengths

### 4. **Structural Innovation**

#### Introduction:
- Starts with stark fact, not generic background
- Uses questions to drive narrative forward
- Identifies specific tensions rather than broad themes
- Clear roadmap without formulaic "Section 2 discusses..."

#### Literature Review:
- **Thematic synthesis** rather than chronological listing
- Integrates competing findings: "While Source A posits X, the empirical evidence in Source B suggests a nuanced deviation..."
- Identifies gaps organically through synthesis, not in separate "gaps" subsection

#### Methodology:
- Hyper-specific details: "Ghana Forestry Commission budget documents report $1,800-$2,200/ha"
- Acknowledges "messy" real-world constraints
- Explicitly discusses limitations in context, not relegated to end

#### Results:
- Direct interpretation: "Eastern region accounts for 26.4% of total deforestation" (then explains significance)
- Avoids formulaic "Figure X shows..." transitions
- Connects findings to implications immediately

#### Discussion:
- Starts with interpretation, not summary
- Discusses political economy constraints honestly
- Uses active voice for policy recommendations
- Ends with practical implementation sequence, not vague "future research needed"

#### Conclusion:
- Begins with decision point, not "In conclusion"
- Synthesizes without repeating
- Acknowledges political dimensions beyond economics
